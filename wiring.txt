Servo Wiring — Jetson Orin Nano Super 40-Pin Header
====================================================

Jetson 40-Pin Header (partial)         Servo Connector
─────────────────────────               ─────────────

  Pin 1  (3.3V)  ○ ● Pin 2  (5V)       ┌───────────┐
  Pin 3  (I2C)   ○ ● Pin 4  (5V) ──────┤ VCC (Red) │
  Pin 5  (I2C)   ○ ● Pin 6  (GND) ─────┤ GND (Brn) │
  Pin 7  (GPIO)  ○ ● Pin 8  (UART)     │           │
  Pin 9  (GND)   ○ ● Pin 10 (UART)     │           │
  ...            ...                     │           │
  Pin 31 (GPIO)  ○ ● Pin 32 (GPIO)     │           │
  Pin 33 (GPIO)  ○─────────────────────┤ Sig (Org) │
  Pin 34 (GND)   ○ ● Pin 35 (I2S)     └───────────┘
  ...            ...
  Pin 39 (GND)   ○ ● Pin 40 (GPIO)


Connections:
  Pin 33 (GPIO) ─── Orange wire ──> Servo Signal
  Pin 4  (5V)   ─── Red wire ─────> Servo VCC
  Pin 6  (GND)  ─── Brown wire ───> Servo GND

Notes:
  - Pin 33 uses BOARD numbering (Jetson.GPIO convention)
  - 5V from Pin 4 can supply a single micro servo (~250mA)
  - For multiple servos or high-torque servos, use an external
    5V supply with common ground to Pin 6
  - Do NOT use Pin 1 (3.3V) for servo power — servos need 5V
