Servo Wiring — Jetson Orin Nano Super 40-Pin Header
====================================================

Jetson 40-Pin Header (partial)       External 5V Supply       Servo Connector
─────────────────────────            ──────────────────       ───────────────

  Pin 1  (3.3V)  ○ ● Pin 2  (5V)
  Pin 3  (I2C)   ○ ● Pin 4  (5V)
  Pin 5  (I2C)   ○ ● Pin 6  (GND) ─────┤ GND ├──────────┤ GND (Brn) │
  Pin 7  (GPIO)  ○ ● Pin 8  (UART)      │     │          │           │
  Pin 9  (GND)   ○ ● Pin 10 (UART)      │ +5V ├──────────┤ VCC (Red) │
  ...            ...                    └─────┘          │           │
  Pin 31 (GPIO)  ○ ● Pin 32 (GPIO)                       │           │
  Pin 33 (GPIO)  ○───────────────────────────────────────┤ Sig (Org) │
  Pin 34 (GND)   ○ ● Pin 35 (I2S)                        └───────────┘
  ...            ...
  Pin 39 (GND)   ○ ● Pin 40 (GPIO)


Connections:
  Pin 33 (GPIO) ─── Orange wire ──> Servo Signal
  External +5V  ─── Red wire ─────> Servo VCC
  External GND  ─── Brown wire ───> Servo GND
  Pin 6  (GND)  ──────────────────> External supply GND (common ground)

Notes:
  - Pin 33 uses BOARD numbering (Jetson.GPIO convention)
  - Recommended: power the servo from a regulated external 5V supply
    with common ground connected to Jetson Pin 6
  - Bench use only: a single micro servo may be powered from Pin 4 if
    startup and stall current are known to be safe. Stall current can
    substantially exceed the roughly 250mA running-current figure
  - Do NOT use Pin 1 (3.3V) for servo power — servos need 5V
